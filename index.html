About Zaken

Most small businesses already sell brilliantly on WhatsApp and Instagram.

What's usually missing isn't a sales pitch, it's one place to point people to. A DM gets buried, a story disappears in a day, and a customer scrolling your feed can't tell what's actually for sale right now.

Zaken gives your shop a single, permanent link. Put it in your bio, your status, or a flyer. Anyone who opens it sees your full catalog and can message you to order, on the app you already use.

Questions

Before you get started

Is Zaken live right now?

Zaken is in early access. If you sign up today, you'll be one of the first shops on the platform, and we'll help you set up your catalog directly.

Do I need a registered business (CAC) to sign up?

No. Zaken is open to any seller, registered or informal. You just need something to sell and a medium customers can reach you on.

Is it free to use?

Yes, creating your store and listing products is free. Any paid features added later will always show their price before you're charged.

Can customers pay me directly through Zaken?

Not yet. Direct payments are on the roadmap.
